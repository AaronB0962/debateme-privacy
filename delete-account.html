Delete your DebateMe account

DebateMe is made by BeachApplications. You can delete your account and the data that goes with it at any time, either inside the app or by emailing us.

Option 1: Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the DebateMe app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Deletion is permanent and happens right away.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you can't get into the app, email beachmadeapps@gmail.com with the subject Delete my DebateMe account.

  • Write from the email address you signed up with, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  • Include your debate name.

We'll delete the account and reply to confirm, within 30 days and usually much sooner.

What is deleted

  • Your account, sign-in details and email address
  • Your profile, bio and profile picture
  • Everything you posted: debates you started, arguments, chat messages, and private messages you sent and received
  • Your thumbs up, reactions, follows and followers, alerts, blocks, and reports you sent

Debates you were part of are deleted too, because a debate can't continue without both sides.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Nothing tied to you is kept after your account is deleted.
  • Our hosting provider may keep routine backups and server logs for a limited time after deletion before they are overwritten.
